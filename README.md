# Loom Video Downloader (Browser Extension) | How to Download Loom Videos

> Browser extension that downloads Loom recordings as MP4 from share links and from Loom players embedded on other websites, including password-protected recordings.

Loom Video Downloader is a browser extension for saving Loom recordings to your computer as MP4. It works on loom.com share pages and on Loom videos embedded in other websites, such as course platforms, docs and community posts, even when the Loom download button is not available to you.

- Save Loom share links and embedded Loom players as MP4
- Works when the Loom download button is missing for viewers
- Password-protected recordings: enter the share password when asked
- Choose a quality and track progress
- No desktop software or command line needed

## Why Loom Video Downloader

Loom makes sharing recordings easy, but viewers often have no download button: the owner may have
disabled downloads, or the recording is embedded inside a course, a help doc or a community post.
Loom streams the video in pieces, so there is no file to right-click and save.

Loom Video Downloader detects the recording on the page you have open, downloads the stream and saves
one MP4, without network traces, page-source inspection or external tools.

## Features

- **Share pages and embeds**: detects Loom recordings on loom.com and Loom players embedded on other websites
- **When download is disabled**: saves recordings your browser can play even when Loom shows no download button
- **Password-protected recordings**: prompts for the share password and uses it only for that session
- **Quality selection**: choose from the qualities Loom provides
- **MP4 output**: one standard video file that plays offline
- **Private by design**: downloads run in your browser; recordings never pass through our servers

## How to Download Loom Videos (Step-by-Step)

1. Install the extension (see [Installation Instructions](#installation-instructions)) and sign in with your email.
2. Open the Loom share link, or the page where the Loom video is embedded.
3. Press play so the recording loads.
4. Click the extension icon. Enter the share password if the recording is protected.
5. Choose a quality and click **Download**. The MP4 is saved in a `Loom` folder in your Downloads.

## Supported Formats

- **Output:** MP4
- **Sources:** loom.com share pages and embedded Loom players
- **Not saved:** Loom's automatic captions and transcripts (download them from Loom separately)

## Troubleshooting

**The recording is not detected**  
Press play first, then reopen the extension. If that does not help, refresh the page and try again.

**A protected recording fails**  
Enter the exact share password the Loom page asks for.

## Installation Instructions

1. Download the ZIP for your browser from the [latest release](https://github.com/serpapps/loom-video-downloader/releases/latest) (for example `loom-downloader-chrome.zip`).
2. Unzip it. You get a folder that contains `manifest.json`.
3. Open your browser's extensions page: `chrome://extensions` (Chrome), `edge://extensions` (Edge), `brave://extensions` (Brave), `opera://extensions` (Opera), `whale://extensions` (Whale) or `browser://extensions` (Yandex).
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.
6. Pin the extension, click its icon, and sign in with your email and the one-time code we send you.

Prefer a guided setup? The [product page](https://serp.ly/loom-video-downloader?via=github) walks you through the same steps.
When a newer version is published, the extension shows an update notice; download the new ZIP from
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/serpapps/loom-video-downloader/releases/latest) and load it the same way.

## FAQ

### How do I download a Loom video?

Open the Loom page or the page with the embedded Loom video, press play, click the extension icon, choose a quality and click **Download**.

### Can I download a Loom video when the owner has disabled downloads?

Yes, if your browser can play the recording. The extension saves what your browser already receives on supported Loom pages and embeds; it does not bypass passwords or workspace permissions.

### Can I download password-protected Loom recordings?

Yes. When you open a protected link, the extension asks for the share password. It uses the password only for that session and never stores it.

### Does it work on embedded Loom videos?

Yes. Loom players embedded on course platforms, help docs and community posts are detected as well as direct Loom links.

### What format are Loom videos saved in?

MP4, which plays on any device without an internet connection.

### Can I save several Loom recordings at once?

The extension handles one recording at a time. Open the next recording and repeat.

### Are captions or transcripts included?

No. Loom's automatic captions are not exported with the video file; download transcripts from Loom separately.

### Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale and Yandex on Windows, macOS and Linux. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers are not supported.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get **3 free downloads a week** with no credit card. A **subscription** gives unlimited downloads, with updates included.

## Notes

- Only download content you own or have permission to save.
- The extension works with videos your own account can already watch in the browser. It does not unlock paid, private or locked content.
